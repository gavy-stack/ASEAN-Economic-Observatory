ASEAN Economic Observatory 

Understanding Indonesia's governance and its position in ASEAN

About

This project is an independent research about analyzing how Indonesia's government manage its country through the lens of institutional quality, public investment, and financial development and relates it across ASEAN.

The question

What makes a government capable of turning its resources and policies into actual results? Because having a budget is one thing. Knowing how to spend it properly is another thing bro 🔥.

Methodology

The project uses publicly available datasets from World Bank, Worldwide Governance Indicators (WGI), and World Justice Project (WJP) and many more.

Python is used for data collection, cleaning, validation, processing, and analysis. Panda library is used to create DataFrame for such purposes.

The initial historical research period is 1996–2024, depending on indicator availability.

⚠️ The project aims to identify patterns and relationships, not to claim that correlation automatically proves causation.
